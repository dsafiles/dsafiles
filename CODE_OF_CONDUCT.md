# Community conduct

Contributors must discuss evidence and edits without harassment, threats, slurs, doxxing, or personal attacks.

Disagreement about sources and wording is expected. Address the claim, citation, method, or scope. Do not speculate about another contributor's identity or motive.

Do not post private information. If a correction or security report contains sensitive material, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a public issue.

Maintainers may close contributions that violate these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.
